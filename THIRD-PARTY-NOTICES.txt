NVIDIA NVAPI headers and interface definitions
-----------------------------------------------

Copyright NVIDIA Corporation and affiliates.
Project: https://github.com/NVIDIA/nvapi
Documentation: https://docs.nvidia.com/nvapi/

The integrated DCC module contains a small managed interoperability definition
for public NVAPI DRS functions and the documented Frame Rate Limiter setting.
NVIDIA SDK binaries and headers are not redistributed in the portable package;
calls are resolved at runtime from the NVIDIA display driver's nvapi64.dll.

The optional experimental NVIDIA driver method additionally resolves an
undocumented JPAC/Max-Perf-per-Watt interface at runtime. It is not presented
as an NVIDIA-supported API, and the option is disabled cleanly when the
installed driver does not expose or accept that interface.
